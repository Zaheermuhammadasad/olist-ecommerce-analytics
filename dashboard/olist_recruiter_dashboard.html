Business insights

  • Late delivery rate increased from 1.50% in 2016 to 9.37% in 2018.
  • Late orders averaged 2.57/5 in review scores versus 4.29/5 for on-time/early orders.
  • Health & Beauty and Watches & Gifts led item-price GMV.
  • Credit cards accounted for the largest share of payment value in the analysed data.
  • Delivery outcomes differed by customer state, so a national average hides local variation.
Definitions: GMV is item price only and excludes freight. Delivery metrics use delivered-status orders with both actual and estimated timestamps; an order is late when actual delivery is strictly later than estimated. Review-score differences are descriptive, not causal. Charts use the analysed dataset and load Plotly from a CDN.
